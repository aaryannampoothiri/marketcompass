Methodology & Transparency

Evaluation Engine & How It Works

A plain-English guide to how Market Compass calculates match scores, analyzes commercial footfall, and helps you make informed location decisions.

🧭

1. What Market Compass Does

Data-driven situational matching between business models and commercial corridors.

Market Compass helps entrepreneurs, business owners, and commercial operators answer two critical location questions with data instead of guesswork:

📍 "I have a place. What business works here?"

Analyzes local customer habits, street layout, and neighborhood competition to recommend the top 3 business concepts most likely to succeed.

💡 "I have a business idea. Where should I open?"

Evaluates all commercial streets across the city to discover the top 3 corridors with the highest matching footfall and unmet demand.

📊

2. The 6 Match Evaluation Dimensions

Every opportunity is scored from 0 to 100 based on six weighted real-world factors.

30% Weight

🏢 Concept & Location Fit

Checks whether the physical space and street format (e.g. walk-in counter, dine-in restaurant, drive-thru) naturally match the commercial character of the street.

25% Weight

👥 Customer Demand & Footfall

Evaluates the presence of your target customers (local residents, morning commuters, office workers, evening socializers) walking through the corridor.

15% Weight

⏰ Best Operating Hours

Analyzes customer footfall during morning commute, midday lunch, evening dinner, and weekend hours to ensure the street is active when your doors are open.

15% Weight

🏪 Competition & Market Space

Calculates unmet customer demand (whitespace) while factoring in nearby corporate chains and beneficial complementary retail co-tenants.

10% Weight

🚶 Accessibility & Walkability

Measures sidewalk flow, proximity to subway and train stations, pedestrian ease, and neighborhood commercial growth momentum.

5% Weight

🏛️ Nearby Attractions & Anchors

Accounts for extra customer surges generated by nearby major transit terminals, universities, sports arenas, or hospitals.

🎯

3. Match Ratings Explained

Clear tiers to help you quickly assess the viability of any opportunity.

STRONG MATCH Score 75 – 100

High synergy across customer demand, operating hours, and location layout. Highly recommended for site visits.

GOOD MATCH Score 60 – 74.9

Viable commercial opportunity with solid customer traffic and healthy market space.

MODERATE Score 45 – 59.9

Moderate demand alignment or specific timing considerations; may require specialized marketing or adapted hours.

RESTRICTED Prerequisite Required

Requires a specific institutional host (e.g. an airport gate concession) not present in this general street location.

💡

4. Practical Advice for Business Operators

Best practices for using market data during your site selection process.

🚶 Always Inspect the Street in Person

Market Compass provides data-driven corridor screening. Once you identify a promising location, visit the street at different times of day (morning commute, midday, and evening) to observe customer footfall in person.

🏪 Understand Your Direct Micro-Neighbors

Look at the immediate block: neighboring businesses like grocery stores, gyms, or popular bakeries often act as positive "anchors" that bring regular everyday foot traffic directly past your doors.

⏰ Align Your Staffing with Peak Hours

Use the recommended operating windows in your detailed report to optimize staff schedules and inventory prep during peak pedestrian commute and lunch windows.
