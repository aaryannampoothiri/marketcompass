Methodology & Transparency

Scoring Engine & Data Governance

How Market Compass calculates opportunity scores, interprets observed supply, and enforces methodological safety.

1. Purpose of Market Compass

Market Compass is a decision-support platform designed to help commercial operators, founders, and brokers evaluate the situational compatibility between business concepts and specific commercial corridors.

The platform addresses two complementary operational questions:

  • Given a Corridor: Which business archetypes have the highest situational fit, timing alignment, and unmet potential?
  • Given a Business Concept: Which commercial corridors offer the strongest audience demand, optimal operating time windows, and favorable access conditions?

2. The 6 Scoring Dimensions & Weights

Every score is calculated deterministically from active dataset fields and normalized strictly from 0 to 100.

Dimension Weight Dataset Fields Used Operational Description
Business & Archetype Fit 30% corridor_archetype_scores.score, archetypes.decision_track, required_gate Measures algorithmic baseline affinity and verifies institutional prerequisites for controlled-host concepts.
Audience Compatibility 25% corridors.audience_scores, audience_confidence, primary_signals Evaluates overlap between archetype customer signals and corridor audience intensity (0–10), weighted by confidence certainty.
Operating-Time Compatibility 15% behavior.daypart_occasion_density, archetypes.demand_clocks Measures footfall density during the concept's active trading windows across 5 standardized dayparts.
Observed Supply & Whitespace 15% behavior.whitespace_quality, brand_ecology.chain_dominance, halo_strength Calculates category whitespace potential and factors in corporate chain saturation vs beneficial co-tenant halo.
Special-Zone Relevance 5% special_zones, host_corridor_ids, zone_type Grants synergy bonuses for proximity to major stadiums, airports, campuses, and hospitals.
Access & Corridor Context 10% access.gateway_dependency, behavior.path_of_travel_friction, transit_car_orientation Evaluates physical circulation friction, transit vs car mode alignment, and neighborhood investment momentum.

3. Fit Tier Classification

STRONG FIT Score 75 – 100

High alignment across primary customer signals, operating hours, and corridor access.

MODERATE FIT Score 60 – 74.9

Viable commercial opportunity with balanced strengths and minor operational tradeoffs.

WEAK FIT Score 45 – 59.9

Low signal alignment or noticeable spatial friction; requires strategic concept adaptation.

GATED OUT Score < 45 / Gate Failure

Fails mandatory operational prerequisites or institutional hosting requirements (e.g. airport concourse in residential area).

4. Methodological Safety & Disclaimers

Observed Supply Standard

Supply and competition metrics in Market Compass reflect observed and sampled business locations in the dataset, not an exhaustive commercial census. Mapped supply indicators should be interpreted as representative samples of commercial density.

Curated Expert Estimates

Behavioral properties (neighborhood momentum, whitespace quality, crime safety perception, timing alpha) represent curated expert evaluations (data_quality.derivation_method = "CURATED_EXPERT_ESTIMATE") designed for scenario screening, not raw sensor feeds.

No Commercial Revenue Guarantees

Opportunity scores measure situational and spatial compatibility. Market Compass does not claim or guarantee customer foot traffic volume, financial revenues, or commercial success.
